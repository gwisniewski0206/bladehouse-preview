Bladehouse

Bladehouse is an electronic music label and event series from Hamburg. It releases vinyl records (BHO001–BHO006) and digital music, hosts club nights in Hamburg, Berlin and Halle at venues including Golden Pudel, Renate, Südpol and Hafenklang, and publishes the Blade'n'Breakfast mix series on SoundCloud (54 episodes).
